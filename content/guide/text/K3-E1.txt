   Carolyn, came to me wanting to make some huge changes in her life. Carolyn
   was feeling stuck. Her space was stagnant and was representing her feelings of
   unworthiness. She had been telling herself an old story of being incapable of
   change or deserving of a better life, and it was reflected in her environment.

   After doing her Heart-Centered Reality Check she was able to identify areas that
   were the sources of frustration and negativity.
   She continued to work through the GUIDE, and made some simple changes that
   completely shifted her thoughts, her space, her home - and subsequently her entire
   life. As a result, Carolyn is now living a life where she feels open to possibilities and
   is even considering moving to the south of France.
