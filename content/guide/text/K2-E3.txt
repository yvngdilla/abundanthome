   Through the focused actions you’ve taken so far, you have a pretty good idea of
   what you are wanting. You have defined your desires, and you’ve created a vision
   board that details those desires.



     Because the Universe works best with clear definitions of your desires, it’s
     important to define those desires in physical form. Creating a style that is

   I want you to use your memory for a moment. Think back to past places or spaces
   you’ve experienced that really left a lasting impression on you. I want you to focus on
   spaces that really gave you those thrill bumps that I keep talking about. For instance,
   maybe you’re remembering a hotel lobby that had the most fantastic vintage couch
   in it, or perhaps a restaurant that had stunning china and cutlery. Or, maybe you’re
   thinking about a music venue that had a truly exquisite ambience. You might even
   be remembering a car that really resonated with you because of its sexy, sleek lines
   and leather interior.

   A lot of very smart people have spent a lot of time developing every detail to evoke
   a certain feeling. Dial into that feeling. Warm, soothing, exciting, spacious, cozy…
   whatever it is that you feel represents your Newlife vision.

                  Trendy Friendy

   Have you ever had a girlfriend that is always “on trend?” She’s copied all the latest design trends,
   but you get the feeling that she has something to prove, or needs to show the world that she’s
   got it all “together.” At a closer glance, you may have also noticed that she is actually miserable.
   You can see that she would rather copy an already socially accepted image of a happy life than
   risk expressing her true self. This friend is always thinking, “If I insert myself into this happy-looking
   space, I will be happy. Right?” I want you to remember that projecting a false sense of wellness is
   not going to fool the Universe.


   There are a variety of different reasons that someone might be more inclined to just “go with the
   flow” of the latest trends. Many of us have self-worth issues, and most of us fear change. Myself
   included! It can be scary to carve your own path in the world. Perhaps we don’t want to appear
   inexperienced, naive, or - god forbid, exposed - for having bad taste! We don’t want to be labeled
   as crass and arrogant, always needing to be the center of attention, something a lady should never
   do. Some of us don’t want to waste money, or cause problems with our partner.

   If any of this sounds familiar - don’t worry! The fact that you are here means that you are ready to
   choose a new and different experience for yourself. I am here to help you cultivate a style that is
   very much reflective of who you are and what you want to create for your future.


   The custom design you are going to create will be uniquely yours, and will reflect your Newlife’s
   journey. This will be an accurate reflection of your soul’s greatest and truest desires - not a staged
   showroom cookie-cutter design! You are not inserting yourself into someone else’s idea of what
   your life should look like!

                  When I have an initial
                  meeting with a client…
   I ask them to wear an outfit that they feel fabulous in - something that really speaks
   to them and represents their Newlife vision. I use that outfit as a springboard into
   building their personal interior design style.

   Take cues from your closet. Do you love to wear all black? Structured suits? Flowy
   neutrals? Big bold patterns? Or maybe jeans and comfy sweaters are more your jam.
   There is also a distinct possibility that you look in your closet and hate what you see
   - so if this is you - think about what your ideal wardrobe would look like. Find images
   in magazines or online that represent that fashion sense. Dress like your boss - and
   your boss is the NEW YOU!

   Compare your NVB with your style description. Does your NVB reflect your Style
   Word? Your Style Word and your NVB should be sympatico!

   If your NVB and your Style Word are not in alignment, your image of yourself may
   have evolved through the process. Make the adjustments NOW, before you move
   any further in the program. Remember, you will get exactly what you are focused on

   thing! So, either make changes to your vision board or to your Style Word, right
   away!

   or a bit disjointed. Further into the design process we will be discussing how to
   coordinate design elements in a manner that creates balance, harmony and
   contrast. These three foundational principles are all you will need to get started on
   creating a design that resonates in the direction of your desires to receive unlimited
   abundance.

   Don’t worry, if while you’re doing this you keep changing your mind or changing
   images - you’re not doing it wrong! You’re not crazy. It’s just that you’re already
   evolving, that’s all. You are fine tuning your vibrational alignment. You’re amazing.
   Look how quickly you’re getting the hang of this! I’m so proud of you!
