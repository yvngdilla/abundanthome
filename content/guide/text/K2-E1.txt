                    LIFE SCRIPT         SAMPLE #1




  In this sample, Jenny is wanting more LOVE:

  “I see myself living a life where I feel loved. I feel loved in a way that is so incredibly
  comfortable. I know that it is easy to love me, and that people want to care for
  me. I enjoy caring for others as well. I love spending time with people. It’s easy
  and effortless for me to open my heart to those I care about. I want more LOVE
  in my life because when I feel loved, I feel good. I feel worthy and excited about
  life. When I feel loved, I feel supported and strong. When I feel love for someone
  else, I feel kind and compassionate. I like to give love and receive love. When I
  feel loved, I like to take care of myself. I like to dress in tailored clothes made of
  fine fabrics. Linen is my favorite fabric. I make good choices with my food. I allow
  myself to sleep as long as I want. Love surrounds me.”


         RESULTS

  In the past, sleeping in was unfathomable to Jenny. She used to think, “Sleeping
  in is what lazy people do. People who sleep in don’t deserve anything good,
  because they are lazy and unwilling to work hard!”

  Well, I say pish posh to that! Once Jenny really got in touch with her desires, she
  discovered that her stories about “people who sleep in” really weren’t rooted in
  reality. When Jenny finally allowed herself to indulge in the pleasures that she
  was desiring in her life, things started to shift for her. She was able to let go of
  the notion that sleeping in was “bad.” She began to access indulgence in her life
  in a way that felt really good to her.
