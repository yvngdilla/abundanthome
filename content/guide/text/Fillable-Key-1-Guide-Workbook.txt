 Welcome!
You’ve taken a powerful step toward designing a life that feels freaking amazing! This
workbook is here to help you take what you’re learning in THE GUIDE TO LIVING IN
ABUNDANCE and turn it into real, tangible shifts in your life. Think of it as your
personal playbook for aligning your mindset, energy, and home so you can manifest
everything you desire (and then some!).

Before we dive into the design work (which is the really fun part!), we need to lay a
strong foundation. This first key is essential—it’s where we clear out the mental
clutter, realign your thoughts, and get you primed for everything that follows. Trust
me, once your mindset is in alignment, the design work will feel effortless and deeply
transformational.

Here’s the big idea behind each key element we’re covering here in this workbook:
   Taming Your Monkey Mind: That noisy, overthinking brain? We’re going to teach
   it to chill out so you can create space for clarity, focus, and a deeper connection
   to Source.
   Tuning Out Negative Thinking: We all have those sneaky negative thoughts that
   try to keep us stuck. Here, we’re rewiring your mental script so you can move into
   a higher vibration and stay there.
   Tuning Into Your Personal Guidance System (PGS): Your senses and emotions
   aren’t just random—they’re your personal GPS for manifestation. You’ll learn how
   to use them to fine-tune your energy and make your home a magnet for
   abundance.
