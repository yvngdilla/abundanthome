   Statement Ceilings
   Wallpaper or paint on the ceiling?! HELL YES! This unconventional and creative design
   choice is a prime option for the adventurous-hearted-master-manifestor. It’s a fantastic
   way to boost manifestations of all kinds. It’s a particularly well-suited choice for rooms
   where you want to spark creativity and playfulness, or create an opulent, luxurious vibe.

   So, go on - I dare ya! Create a bold statement by painting or wallpapering above your
   head! You can even combine this idea with an accent wall to elevate the ceiling and
   elongate the room (I’ll explain more about this later on in this Element).

   Opting for lighter colors on the ceiling can create a sense of openness, making it seem
   higher, while darker hues can bring a cozy and intimate feel to the space. Just a little tip:
   if you’re dealing with a smaller room, be mindful of dark colors. They can make the room
   feel a bit tight if not used thoughtfully.

   Adding wood to your ceiling is an excellent way to draw attention to it and infuse your space
   with a natural warmth and richness. While it’s a bit more complex than simply painting or
   wallpapering, if you have the resources to get it professionally installed (or if you’re skilled
   enough to do it yourself), it’s definitely worth considering. Make sure to choose wood
   tones that either match or complement your furniture to create a harmonious flow, and
   keep in mind the impact your existing flooring will have on your ceiling.

                       PAINT IT BLACK

   One of my top picks for walls, whether it’s with paint or wallpaper, is black! It makes
   everything on or in front of it pop without overwhelming the objects themselves.

   It’s the perfect backdrop!




ABUNDANT HOME
                                       PAINT & WALLPAPER



   Many people worry that dark hues like black might give a room a gloomy feel, but from
   my experience, it has quite the opposite effect. Black and other dark colors often bring
   a sense of grounding and support to a space, creating an environment that feels stable
   and comforting.

   Can black look intense or goth? Sure. And make no mistake, those can be very cool looks,
   but keep in mind that using black paint or wallpaper in your space can also add a high
   level of sophistication and elegance.

   Black enhances the vibrancy of colors and gives a strong background for artwork. It adds
   not just visual depth and character to a room, but also cultivates a cozy and intimate
   feelling. When you incorporate black walls in your space, it opens it up for opportunities
   to make bolder, more adventurous decor choices that you may have never considered
   before.
   To be clear, black is as diverse and intriguing as any other color out there! Black comes
   in an array of shades and undertones that can dramatically change the mood and style
   of a space. You can find blacks with cool blue undertones, giving a sharp and modern
   edge. Or blacks with warm red undertones, offering a more inviting and cozy feel. There
   are even blacks tinged with green or purple, each adding a unique depth and character.
   These subtle variations in undertone can impact how the color interacts with light and
   other elements in your room, creating different atmospheres from sleek and sophisticated,
   to warm and enveloping.

   So if you’ve been thinking about black for your space, don’t be afraid to use it - if it aligns
   with your vision. Just make sure you are using your big picture perspective, inside and
   out.

     TO PAINT OR NOT TO PAINT…
   Painting isn’t for everyone! And that’s ok. Make sure you get clear on whether or not
   you’re going to incorporate this process into your design journey. Will you paint one
   wall, one room, your front door…or the whole house, inside and out?

   If you’re going to hire a professional to help you, do your research now and make
   sure you choose a person or a company that you trust.

   Just know that you don’t have to do everything all at once! It’s ok to take baby steps,
   especially with big projects like painting. Take your time making a decision, and do
   what feels right for you at this time.

   FIRST, FINISH STRONG

   For starters, there are a few different styles of paint finishes out there - and trust me -
   you want to be intentional about choosing the right one for the right space.

   The finish you choose can affect your paint color - so it’s good practice to finalize the
   finish you want in each space and area before choosing the actual colors.

   Here’s a guide to the most common finishes and where best to use them:

   • Flat/Matte: No shine, absorbs light. Ideal for ceilings and low-traffic areas, hides
   imperfections well. There are some washable flats that are a little bit more durable.
   • Eggshell: Slight sheen, more durable than flat. Good for moderate-traffic areas like
   living rooms.
   • Satin: Similar to Eggshell, but a more velvety sheen, easy to clean. Great for high-
   traffic areas like kitchens and bathrooms.
   • Semi-Gloss: Shiny, highly durable. Perfect for trim, cabinets, and areas that need
   frequent cleaning.
   • High-Gloss: Very shiny, most durable. Ideal for accents and surfaces that endure
   a lot of wear.

   PRIMED AND PREPPED

   Usually, you don’t need a primer when painting over an existing painted surface. But for new,
   unpainted surfaces (like exposed drywall or freshly plastered areas), priming is a necessary
   step.

   Priming these kinds of surfaces ensures good paint adhesion, prevents stains, and helps
   achieve a long-lasting finish. This is especially true for challenging projects that deal with
   high absorbency, color changes, glossy or oil-based surfaces, peeling paint, mold-prone
   areas, or wood with tannin bleed.

   There are also plenty of paint-and-primer combos out there that simplify the process and
   save time, though they might cost a bit more.

   TEST DRIVE

   Have you ever fallen in love with a paint color, only to discover it looks completely different
   once it’s on your walls? Yeah, we’ve all been there.

   Picking out paint swatches is an exciting first step in the process, but if you want to save
   yourself from some heartache (and headache!), there’s a golden rule you should follow:

   Always, always, ALWAYS test your paint before committing to it.

   Before you decide on what color to paint, here’s a step-by-step guide to help you get it just
   right:

      K.I.S.S. Your Color Choices: When it comes to sample paint, you want options - but
      not too many! Aim for at least two colors, but no more than four. Take a grounded
      and balanced approach by carefully narrowing your selection down to a few solid
      options before purchasing your sample paint.

      Purchase Your Sample Paint: Paint samples don’t always perfectly match the color
      as it appears on swatches, so I always recommend that you sample the actual paint
      in the finish you’ve selected on the walls in your space.

      Test Your Paint: Test your sample paint by actually painting a square on your wall. The
      larger the sample, the better—I find that 8” x 8” or 12” x 12” squares work well. Make sure
      you paint a sample swatch on every wall of the room you’re going to paint.

ABUNDANT HOME
                                      PAINT & WALLPAPER



      Let it Dry: Make sure you let the paint dry before you decide! It will look totally different
      when it’s wet.

      Observe the Lighting Throughout the Day: Once the paint has dried, take the time to
      observe it throughout the day in natural light. Paint colors aren’t static. Notice how the
      color changes with the shifting daylight.

      In the evening, look at the swatches again under artificial lighting. Ensure that you still
      love the color under these conditions, as the ambiance will be vastly different than
      during the day.

      Pull it All Together: Get out your Project Board and cross-reference any fabric,
      wallpaper, or pattern and texture materials you will be using against your paint. You can
      tape or nail them to the paint samples or the wall.

      Reflect and Decide: Don’t rush your decision. I always say, take a risk and give it 2
      weeks before you decide if you love or hate it. Live with the swatches for a few days.
      Sit with the colors, observe them during various times, and see how they resonate
      with your space and mood (Maybe even have a glass of wine with your girlfriends and
      analyze it!). Visualize your furniture and accessories in the space. How does it feel?

  If you’re hesitant to apply the actual paint directly to your chosen surface, there’s good
  news - many paint companies now offer larger sample swatches that are easy to apply
  and remove. While I still highly recommend testing the actual paint for optimal results, I
  understand that it might not always be feasible. In such cases, these larger samples are
  your next best option, providing a practical alternative to help you make the right choice
  for your space.

  Here’s a friendly reminder: DON’T WORRY! IT’S ONLY PAINT!

  There have been too many tears shed over which shade of beige to use. Don’t add any
  unnecessary stress to yourself or your life. The beauty of paint lies in its versatility—it can
  always be painted over if you change your mind or make a mistake. So let’s keep things
  light, and enjoy the process!

  Struggling to make a decision? Or maybe just want some feedback? Make sure to touch
  base with your fellow Co-Creators in the Abundant Home Collective, a great resource to
  ask questions, gain insight, and be a part of the Master Manifestor community!

  Just like with wallpaper, getting creative with how and where you paint in your home can
  give you the ability to create illusions within your space. Paint possesses a magical quality
  that extends beyond mere color. It can act as a magician’s wand - conjuring up illusions,
  reshaping environments, and charming the eye with delightful tricks.

  Have you ever walked into a room with soaring, cathedral-like ceilings that seemed to
  stretch into a vast, impersonal expanse? Or maybe you’ve encountered the opposite
  problem, where a space has felt uncomfortably low and cramped.

  The incredible thing about paint is that whether you want to make a room appear more
  spacious, cozier, taller, or simply more inviting, the right paint techniques can work wonders
  to bring your vision to life.

  Here are a few of my favorite techniques to use paint with purpose and intention in your
  space:

   Color drenching is a method of painting
   where a single color is used on walls,
   ceilings, doors, and trims. This is an
   immersive approach that creates a
   unified look, by blurring boundaries
   and minimizing visual interruptions.
   Depending on the chosen color, it can
   either create a dramatic, modern feel, or
   a serene, harmonious atmosphere. Matte
   or flat paint is often used in this technique
   for a super-cool modern edge. However,
   this can work with different sheens. If you
   like the flat/matte look but decide to try
   this out in an active area, you may want
   to consider painting the trim in the same
   color, but a different finish like satin or
   eggshell to make it more durable.

                                THE BASICS
   One of the things I love most about wallpaper is how wonderfully it can add depth to a
   space. Even better, it always creates a special touch of originality (especially in the case of
   patterned wallpapers!).

   There are SO many versatile ways that wallpaper can be used to achieve various looks
   in every room of the house. Wallpaper can really give a space that “aha” feeling in even
   the most basic of rooms. It adds character to a space with its elements of color, style, and
   texture.

   Rule number one: As with big paint projects, using wallpaper is not for everyone! This
   decision of whether or not to use wallpaper in your design takes careful and intentional
   consideration.

   But, assuming you’ve thought things through and want to move forward, here’s a few basics
   of wallpaper to assist you in this part of your design journey!

  SO MANY OPTIONS
  Not all wallpaper is created equal! There are a variety of different styles and textures, and
  choosing the right one for you will depend on your unique desires, preferences, budget,
  and lifestyle. As with the decision to use wallpaper in the first place, take your time with this
  part of the process to ensure you are landing on the right choice for you, your life, and your
  manifestations.

      Traditional wallpaper is the standard option, requiring adhesive for application and
      available in a wide array of patterns, textures, and finishes.
      Vinyl wallpaper, on the other hand, is durable and moisture-resistant, making it
      suitable for high-traffic areas and easy to clean.
      Textured wallpaper adds depth and dimension to the walls with raised patterns or
      textures.
      Grasscloth wallpaper, crafted from natural materials like grasses, offers an organic
      and textured appearance, providing a unique and earthy feel.
      Embossed wallpaper features raised patterns or designs, introducing a tactile element
      to the walls.
      Mural wallpaper showcases large-scale designs or scenic vistas, often used to create
      a focal point.
      Linen wallpaper mimics the texture of linen fabric, offering a subtle and sophisticated
      look.
      Washable wallpaper is resistant to water and moisture, making it easy to clean and
      maintain.
      Metallic wallpaper incorporates metallic elements for a shimmering and contemporary
      effect.
      Cork wallpaper, made from cork material, adds warmth and texture to the walls and is
      eco-friendly.
      Flocked wallpaper, covered with a raised velvet-like pattern, brings a luxurious and
      tactile quality.
      Anaglypta wallpaper, featuring intricate and repeating patterns, is often used to
      achieve a vintage or Victorian aesthetic.

  IT’S ONLY TEMPORARY: Removable Options

      Peel and stick wallpaper is the go-to choice for temporary or rental spaces. Its self-
      adhesive properties make it easy to apply and remove, making it the most convenient
      option out there.

       COVERING ALL YOUR BASES
   You know, wallpapers can be tricky. They often look a bit different in person than they do on
   your screen or in a catalog. That’s where sampling comes in handy!

   Just like with paint, it’s super important to test out your wallpaper choices before making a
   final decision. While it’s similar to sampling paint, there are a few wallpaper-specific tips to
   consider before diving in:


        1. Color Accuracy
        2. Texture and Material
        3. Dye Lot Variations
        4. Overall Effect (Scale)
        5. Multiple Samples

  It’s all about checking out the real-deal color right in your own space, under your own lights.
  Just like with paint, you want to make sure that you are looking at your sample throughout
  the day, in different light conditions to see if it meets your expectations.

  And textures? They’re a big deal with wallpaper. A sample lets you actually touch and feel
  the texture and see how it plays with the light in your room.

  PRO TIP: wallpapers are printed in batches called dye lots, and colors can vary a tad
  between these batches. Sampling first means you can be sure the color is just what you
  want before you order a whole bunch. If you like the sample, jot down its dye lot number!!
  That way, you can ask for the same batch when you buy. This will also help to keep your walls
  consistent if you need to buy more.

  If you can’t find a dye lot number on your wallpaper sample, look instead for a batch or run
  number. Some companies use these terms interchangeably with “dye lot.” It’s just their way
  of tracking the batches, and it’s super important for making sure you get the exact same
  color and pattern in every roll you order.

                   COMA COMA COMA
                     CHAMELEON

   Did you know that wallpaper patterns can be quite the chameleons? Some have small
   designs that you can see repeated several times on a single swatch. Others have these big,
   bold patterns where you only get a snippet on the swatch, leaving a bit to your imagination.
   When it comes to wallpaper, scale matters, and sometimes it’s hard to visualize what a full
   wall (much less a full room) of wallpaper will look like from a small sample. There will often be
   a room scene on the back of the sample to help interpret the full scale pattern.

   So here’s a little trick that might help you decide:


   Take that swatch and stick it on your wall.
   Then, step back as far as you can (maybe
   even check it out from the doorway). This
   little step can make a huge difference. It
   helps you to get some perspective and
   see whether the pattern vibes with what
   you are going for in your space. You might
   find that a small, subtle pattern doesn’t
   quite make the statement you imagined
   - or that maybe a large, loud design feels
   too overwhelming for your space.

      PARTY, AND YOU’RE INVITED




   Finally, when it comes to wallpaper, the more samples, the merrier! Unlike paint, where
   sometimes a few swatches does the trick, wallpaper really shines when you have a variety to
   choose from. Order a bunch of samples and mix and match them in your space. It’s amazing
   how patterns and colors can interact with each other in your own room. Sometimes, the
   wallpaper you were sure would be THE ONE ends up taking a back seat to a new and
   unexpected favorite!

   And just a little reminder: if you’re pairing wallpaper with paint, pick your wallpaper first! This
   makes choosing paint colors way easier because you’ll be able to “pull” your paint colors
   out from your sample.

   As always, don’t forget to cross-check your wallpaper samples with all the other elements
   you’ve picked out. Everything needs to play nicely together to create that perfect harmony
   in your space. Just like putting together a great outfit – everything has got to work together!

        RENTER-FRIENDLY
   ALTERNATIVES TO WALLPAPER
           AND PAINT
  Being a renter or living in a temporary space doesn’t have to be a barrier to incorporating
  paint or wallpaper into your home. Here’s a list of simple alternatives that are both easy to
  implement and remove (and are often more cost-effective):

        Peel and Stick Wallpaper: Instead of installing traditional wallpaper, use peel and
        stick options that are not only affordable, but provide the ability to transform any
        space with ease. You literally peel, and stick! Let the Universe do the heavy lifting!
        Oh yeah, baby!

        Frame It: If covering walls feels like too much of a commitment, or if you’ve found a
        pattern that would be overwhelming if it covered an entire wall – use it somewhere
        else! Framing a panel of wallpaper is an incredible low-commitment way to incorporate
        the magic of pattern, texture, and color into your space.

        Panels/Canvases: This same technique can be applied to a paint alternative.
        Purchase some large canvases from your local art store and paint them in solid colors
        of your choice. Depending on the size and amount of panels you use, you can achieve
        similar results of painting an entire wall. Panels give you the flexibility to experiment
        with placement and to move things around as your desires shift and expand.

        Tapestries and fabrics: If your landlord would flip out if they saw a brightly painted
        or wallpapered room - skillfully hanging a beautiful tapestry or piece of fabric in your
        space can be an amazing alternative (We’ll also be doing a deep dive in the “Artwork”
        Element later on).

        Painting Alternatives: Consider painting things like plant holders, lampshades,
        frames for your artwork, or furniture - to seriously elevate the mood and vibration of
        your space.
