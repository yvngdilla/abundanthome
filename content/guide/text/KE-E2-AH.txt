                  When creating your
                  Newlife Vision Board




       The goal is to create visual triggers that stimulate your
     vibrational focus specifically towards your Newlife desires.


   NVB doesn’t even have to be an actual board. It just needs to be something
   that you have easy access to and that you can use to easily remind yourself of
   your Newlife vision.
   I know many people who like to create “vision notebooks.” It is just like a vision
   board, but it’s in a journal, and you can continue to add to it in a fluid and ongo-
   ing manner.

   This notebook could be full of ideas, scenarios and images, and a copy of your
   Newlife Script. You should keep this notebook on your nightstand to review in
   bed as you’re falling asleep, or as soon as you wake up in the morning. What a
   great way to start your day!

        As you begin to work on your
         NVB or Vision Notebook...
Start by collecting fabrics and images of objects that express the connection to the emotions
that are important to you. You want these images and choices to reflect your Newlife Vision.
You can even spray perfume or rub an essential oil on it! Feel free to get creative.

Your NVB can be 3D! Do not feel limited by
the flat surface you are working with. Feel free
to glue things onto it as you see fit. It doesn’t
have to fit within a frame or be tidy (unless you
want it that way, of course!).

I remember being a student in one of my
first interior design classes. We were asked
to create a vision board / materials board for
a project. Well, everybody came in with their
tidy little boards, with all of their materials
perfectly laid out in grid format with beautiful
labels and index keys. Mine was twice the size
of everyone else’s, had sticks and feathers
jutting out from every angle, and even had
pieces of frames and jewelry dangling from
the edges! It was magnificent! The teacher
was pretty flabbergasted, and didn’t quite
know what to do with me. To be honest - many
people still don’t know what to do with me -
and I like it that way! My creative process is
one of my greatest strengths. Ultimately, I quit
the class and a year later ended up being a
guest lecturer :o)

                 Are you ready?
   Here are some simple steps to follow to actually create your very special and very unique Newlife
   Vision Board!


             Gather your supplies: Acquire the foundation for your board - a corkboard, poster board,
             or a digital platform like Pinterest or Canva. Collect magazines, physical photos, images,
       1     quotes, and any other materials that resonate with your desires. You can also print out
             images from the internet.


             Reinforce your Vision: Read over your Newlife Script again, and connect with the
      2      vibration of your desires.


             Visualization: Take some time to visualize yourself actually living your Newlife. Take a
             moment to let yourself really feel the emotions associated with your desires. Envision
       3     feelings, surroundings, colors, smells, emotions…leave no stone unturned! Make the
             experience real for yourself.


             Select and arrange images: Look through your collection of images and choose the
             ones that are most aligned and that represent your Newlife Vision. These could be

       4     pictures of places you want to visit, items you want to own, or images that evoke the
             feelings you want to experience. Arrange these images on your vision board in a way
             that feels visually pleasing to you.


             Add affirmations and quotes: Incorporate your own personalized written affirmations

      5      and / or meaningful quotes onto your vision board. Use colorful markers or pens to write
             them directly on the board, or print them out and attach them.

              Make it visually appealing: Enhance the visual appeal of your vision board by adding
              colors, patterns, and decorations that resonate with you. Feel free to use stickers,
              ribbons, markers, colored pencils, glitter, or any other artistic elements to make it visually

       6      captivating. You can also use the principles of design here! Remember that you can use
              Balance, Symmetry, Asymmetry, Radial Balance…let this process be intuitive, with the
              intention of making this board a pleasure to look at! Access the Sensory Chart, Color
              Wheel and Aroma Wheel in the Resource tab to align with specific components.


              On Display: Find a prominent place in your home where you can display your vision
              board. It could be in your bedroom, office, or any other location where you will see
       7      it every day. Make sure it’s easily visible and accessible so you can connect with it
              regularly.


              Review and visualize daily: Set aside a few minutes each day to review your vision board.

       8      Take a moment to visualize your Newlife, in detail, as if it has already come to fruition.
              Feel the embodied emotions associated with your desires. This practice will help you
              stay aligned with your vision.


              Update and evolve: As you move forward in your life and continue on this journey, your
              Newlife vision may change. Remain flexible and open to updating and evolving your
       9      vision board accordingly. Remove anything that doesn’t feel in alignment anymore and
              add new things that express your ever-evolving desires.




   Remember, a vision board is a visual representation of your Newlife. This incredible resource - in
   addition to consistent focus, belief, and inspired action - is what will ultimately create the results
   you desire.
