                   ACCESSORY BASICS
 Your space will flow nicely when your accessories reflect your design style and color
 scheme. But if you’re into a mix of styles (like most of us), that’s fine too! Just make sure
 that there’s something that ties your eclectic pieces together.

 Here are 5 steps to decorate using your accessories with confidence.

 1. Perspective: Start by thinking about how this object or grouping will be viewed. Is it a
    360 view, or a straight-on perspective? Make sure you walk around the grouping and
    view it from all angles.
 2. Large Anchor Piece: Begin by placing larger decor items as anchors for your overall
    arrangement. These could be items like sizable vases, big books, or statement
    sculptures. They act as focal points and help ground your arrangement.
 3. Layer with Medium-Sized Items: Next, add medium-sized items such as smaller
    framed photos, decorative bowls, or smaller plants. These should complement your
    anchor pieces without overwhelming them. Place them in front of, beside, or on top of
    the larger items to create depth.
 4. Incorporate Small Accent Pieces: Add small trinkets or decorative objects to fill in
    the gaps. These can be items like tiny figurines, small candles, or decorative boxes.
    They add interest and detail to your shelves. Just make sure not to overdo it, or they
    can get too cluttered and messy.
 5. Play with Heights and Textures: To create a dynamic look, vary the heights and
    textures of the items. Stack books or use stands to elevate items. Mix materials like
    metal, glass, wood, and fabric to add texture and visual appeal.
 6. Think About Negative Space: Avoid clutter by allowing some empty space. This
    negative space helps to keep an area from looking overcrowded, and lets each piece
    stand out more distinctly.

 There's something about open spaces that makes people want to fill them! I always
 get a giggle after my housekeeping crew has been to my home. They always take my
 accessories and carefully spread them out along the surface making sure they are evenly
 spaced in straight lines. I have to go around and regroup everything!

  Use this process to build your accessory focal points. Be sure to keep in mind that your
  goal is to always create balance, whether that is on a mantel, bookcase display, or in an
  environment within a room (or throughout the house).

  PRO TIPS:

  • Pay attention to scale and proportion – mixing objects that are too similar in size or too
      spaced out might not look balanced.
  •   To achieve a cohesive and balanced look in your display, consistently repeat colors
      throughout, creating a treasure trail of sensory triggers. Be sure and consult the color
      wheel for specific emotional relationships to the colors you use.
  •   Simplify your color palette. By limiting the range of colors, you’ll find it easier to create a
      display that looks both sophisticated and expertly curated.
  •   Consoles, coffee and side tables, nightstands, and countertops are prime spots for
      telling your Newlife story through accessories and decor.
  •   Use a decorative tray to corral and organize daily items. Recall how you can utilize active
      and passive zones (Remember Key 3, Element 3: Mindful Storage?) and let that guide
      your accessory choices.
  •   Do you need a small decorative bowl to sit atop the table by the front door to place your
      keys as you enter your home, or perhaps a dish to collect your jewelry at night? Will some
      decorative baskets sitting at the bottom of your console table help to keep you stylishly
      organized? These are all great questions to ask yourself as you are brainstorming.

  But here’s the real and raw truth when it comes to styling accessories…even the pros
  struggle sometimes!

  You know those picture-perfect arrangements in magazines and on your social feeds?
  Well, they often take time (and often multiple tries) to get it right. Not to mention – those
  arrangements are great for photoshoots, but don’t always translate into a functional space
  that you could live in. Trust me on that!!!

  Likely, the designer isn’t even the one creating the picture-perfect vignettes! Prop stylists
  are often hired to add the final touches that make these spaces memorable. It’s truly a
  specialized skill that most of us have to cultivate and hone over time.

  So, if your first attempt at styling doesn’t quite hit the mark, keep tweaking. Play with balance,
  proportion, scale, and the golden Rule of Three. And remember to Keep It Simple,
  Superstar! With a bit of patience and creativity, you’ll create a space that’s not just visually
  stunning, but powerful, too.

        GUESS WHO’S COMING TO
         DINNER: TABLESCAPES




   Are you a dinner party girl??

   Or maybe you just like to invite the gals over for a little wine-nosh-charcuterie action
   to unwind after a busy day.

   Either way, large tabletops are prime real estate for fast-tracking manifestation magic,
   and making your space look and feel really beautiful.

   You can have a lot of fun here and get really creative! And as I always say, good
   design does not have to cost a lot of money, so you don’t have to go out and buy a
   bunch of stuff to make magic happen.

   Go scouting around your house and use items that you already have. Add decor like
   potted plants, trays, and candles.

   Repurpose old wine bottles and glasses for candlesticks and votives for a light-
   hearted, old-world feeling. Cut branches from your tree and stick them in an urn for
   dramatic flair. Use mismatched plates or glasses for a funky and creative vibe. Display
   fruit or vegetables as a symbol of abundance. Or, you can even lay parchment paper
   down the center as a runner so guests can write their names at their seats to inject
   a sense of humor and fun!
ABUNDANT HOME
                                ACCESSORIES - CAKE STORY




   Really, the options are endless.

   Again, you’ll want to layer, so think about your design principles (like balance and
   scale) and always keep what you are wanting to manifest in the forefront of your mind
   to guide your design.

   Here are some helpful tips on how to build your tablescapes and become the
   MARTHA STEWART of the LOA:

   1. Start with a Theme or Color Scheme: Decide on a theme or a color palette that
      will guide your choices for table decor. It could be seasonal, formal, casual, or
      based on a specific color or style.
   2. Choose Your Tablecloth or Runner: Begin by laying down the foundation. If you
      want to use one, choose a tablecloth or a runner that aligns with your theme. A
      tablecloth provides a classic look, while a runner can add a modern touch. Or –
      layer them both!
   3. Decide on a Centerpiece: The centerpiece is your focal point of the table. It could
      be a floral arrangement, plants, candles, a decorative object, or a combination
      of these. Remember to keep it at a height that allows people to see each other
      across the table.
   4. Choose Your Place Settings: Lay out the plates next. You can start with a charger
      plate, followed by the dinner plate and then the salad plate on top if your meal
      requires it.
   5. Get Clear on Cutlery and Glassware: There are so many ways to approach this.
      For more formal occasions, the general rule is to place forks to the left of the plate,
      and knives and spoons to the right. The dessert spoon or fork can be placed
      horizontally above the plate. Arrange the glassware to the top right of the dinner
      plate, starting with a water glass and then wine glasses if serving wine.
   6. Think About Napkins: Fold or roll napkins and place them on top or underneath
      the plates or in the glasses. You can also use napkin rings too.
   7. Play With Place Cards: If it’s a formal dinner or you have a seating plan, place
      cards can be added above the dessert spoon or on the napkins. This is one of
      those things where you can get super creative and have a lot of fun.
   8. Consider Ambiance and Lighting: Add candles, or fairy lights for a warm and
      inviting glow.

                         MAGIC MIRROR
  Mirror mirror on the wall, who’s the fairest of them all???

  Mirrors truly are magical. They’ve got it all! They’re not only visually stunning, but are
  also functional and transformative. In the world of design and decorating, they’re a
  real triple threat.

  But what about manifesting?

  Using mirrors in your home for manifestation is a powerful technique. Here’s how you
  can use them in your home to super-charge your desires:

  • Reflecting Your Goals: Position mirrors to reflect symbols of your goals or
      aspirations. For instance, if you’re aiming for abundance, you might place a
      mirror where it reflects a bowl of fruit or a painting that symbolizes prosperity. The
      reflection doubles the presence of these symbols in your space, reinforcing your
      intention.
  •   Creating a Space of Affirmation: Get your Stewart Smalley on and use mirrors
      as a tool for daily affirmations. Positioning a mirror in a place where you frequently
      spend time allows for moments of self-affirmation. As you pass by or gaze into the
      mirror, affirm your goals and desires. This repeated positive reinforcement can
      be a powerful manifestation tool. Remember, you’re good enough, you’re smart
      enough, and doggonit…people like you ; )
  •   Amplifying Positive Energy: Mirrors can be used to amplify positive energy in
      your space. Placing a mirror in a location that reflects a beautiful view or a favorite

   From a design perspective, mirrors add a bit of jewelry to the room with their reflective
   surface. And because they are available in an array of styles, they can act as a stunning
   focal point that complement various interior decor themes.

   One of a mirror’s greatest features is their ability to amplify light. By reflecting both
   natural and artificial light, mirrors can instantly brighten up a room and make it feel
   more fresh and inviting, making them a must-have for anyone who lacks sufficient
   natural lighting in their home.

   But perhaps their most celebrated trick is how they make spaces feel bigger. Mirrors
   invite depth and an illusion of expansiveness, turning cramped rooms into seemingly
   spacious areas. This trick works wonders in narrow hallways and snug rooms. You can
   use a single mirror or create a mirror wall of them, and see how your room feels like it
   has doubled in size!

   Whether you’re opting for a grand full-length mirror in your bedroom, choosing an
   ornate piece above the fireplace, or arranging a collection of smaller mirrors for an
   artistic touch, mirrors offer a blend of functionality, beauty, and style.
